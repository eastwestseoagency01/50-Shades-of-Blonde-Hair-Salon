What We Offer

Our Hair Services

From color to treatment, every service is performed by expert hairdressers using premium products at our Miami hair salon.

Balayage Hair

Balayage is the art of painting color directly onto the hair in a sweeping, freehand motion to create naturally sun-kissed, lived-in color. Unlike traditional foiling, balayage produces a softer, more blended result that grows out beautifully with minimal maintenance. Our skilled hairdressers in Miami customize every balayage to complement your skin tone, natural hair color, and personal style - whether you prefer a subtle, barely-there brightening or a dramatic, high-contrast transformation.

Book Balayage

Brazilian Keratin Treatment

Say goodbye to frizz and hello to smooth, glossy, manageable hair. Our Brazilian keratin treatment infuses the hair shaft with a rich keratin formula that eliminates frizz, reduces curl, and leaves hair feeling silky and healthy. Miami's humid climate is famous for causing frizz, making keratin treatment one of our most popular services. Results can last between three and five months, and the treatment works on all hair types - curly, wavy, coarse, and chemically treated. Walk out with the sleek, polished hair you have always wanted.

Book Keratin Treatment

Blonde Highlights

Our blonde highlights service is all about adding brightness, dimension, and life to your hair. Whether you want a few subtle face-framing pieces or a full head of bright, beachy blonde, our Miami hairdressers will tailor the placement and tone to perfectly suit your face shape and complexion. We use a range of techniques including foiling and balayage to create highlights that look natural and effortless. The result is hair that catches the light beautifully and gives you that radiant, sun-lit look year round.

Book Highlights

Straightening Hair

If you are dreaming of smooth, poker-straight hair without the daily battle with a flat iron, our professional hair straightening service is exactly what you need. Our hairdressers take great care to protect your hair's integrity throughout the process, applying nourishing treatments alongside the straightening formula to keep your hair healthy, shiny, and strong. Enjoy weeks or even months of effortless, straight hair with our expert straightening services in Miami.

Ombre Hair

Ombre creates a seamless gradient effect where color transitions from darker roots to lighter ends, producing a bold and visually striking result. This technique is incredibly versatile - it can be subtle and natural or dramatic and high-contrast depending on your preference. Our Miami hairdressers are experts at creating ombre looks that are perfectly blended and customized to your desired color palette. Whether you want classic brunette-to-blonde ombre or something more creative, we can make it happen with skill and precision.

Book Ombre

Partial Highlights

Partial highlights are a targeted approach to adding color that focuses on specific sections of the hair - typically the top, crown, and face-framing layers. This is an excellent option for clients who want to refresh their look, add brightness around the face, or maintain their existing highlights without committing to a full head of color. Partial highlights are also more time-efficient and budget-friendly, making them a popular choice at our Miami hair salon for clients looking for a natural brightness boost between full color appointments.

Book Partial Highlights

Lowlights

Lowlights add depth, richness, and dimension by weaving in tones that are darker than your base color. They are the perfect counterbalance to highlights and are often used together to create a multidimensional, natural-looking result. Lowlights are also ideal for clients who want to tone down overly bright or brassy color, or simply add warmth and richness back into hair that has faded. Our Miami hairdressers expertly blend lowlights into your existing color for a seamless, beautifully dimensional finish.

Book Lowlights

Hair Painting

Hair painting is a freehand technique where color is literally brushed or painted directly onto the hair, giving the colorist complete artistic control over placement, blend, and intensity. The result is an incredibly natural, personalized look that is uniquely yours. Unlike foiling, there are no harsh lines or regrowth lines to worry about - the color blends beautifully as it grows out. Our expert hairdressers at 50 Shades of Blonde use hair painting to create custom looks ranging from subtle, dimensional color to bold, creative color artistry in Miami.

Book Hair Painting
